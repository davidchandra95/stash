**What works**

- The document leads. Title, headings, body text, and muted metadata have distinct roles.
- Settings have clear categories, search, previews, and automatic-save guidance. Their narrow layout stays reachable.
- Desktop stays compact, while mobile has larger controls and a reachable New note action.

**Priority issues**

1. **[P2] Search scope is unclear.** In Work, entering garden in Search your notes produces No notes found. Entering the same word in Find notes finds A garden journal in Learning. Users must learn that the first field filters the current view while the top field searches titles across notebooks. Label them Filter this view and Find note titles, make content-search scope visible, and add a Search all notes route in the empty state. Suggested command: $impeccable clarify.

2. **[P2] Short notebook names truncate too early.** At 900px, Personal and Learning shorten even while their action buttons are hidden. Source confirms both hidden actions still occupy layout width. Keep current target sizes and keyboard access, but give labels useful space through an adaptive narrow-row treatment. Verify idle, hover, focus, and open-menu states together. Suggested command: $impeccable layout.

3. **[P2] Note lists provide too little recognition context.** Desktop shows a title and timestamp; mobile shows only a title and pin. Similar titles force users to open notes to tell them apart. Offer one optional preview line and notebook context in All notes. Keep the compact list as a choice. Suggested command: $impeccable shape.

4. **[P3] Formatting controls have too little visual grouping.** At 1280px, roughly two dozen controls occupy one strip. At narrow widths, overflow already helps. Improve grouping by purpose and keep less frequent insertion actions in More. Preserve the required text-style, Bold, Italic, Underline, Undo, and Redo controls. Suggested command: $impeccable distill, then $impeccable polish.

**Cognitive load and emotional journey**

Three checklist concerns: weak grouping of the large toolbar, many equally visible formatting choices, and remembering note contents to distinguish sparse list rows. The writing experience feels calm. Friction rises during retrieval and narrow navigation. Search recovery is the clearest opportunity to restore confidence. The skill's four-option guideline is used as a prompt for review, not a reason to remove useful app navigation.
