Stash for Android, v2

The desktop v2 language with touch geometry: the same tokens, color roles, accent and light/dark, at 48px action targets and 44px+ rows and fields. Type uses 16px for content and 13–14px for secondary text.

  1. Mobile loads the v2 stylesheets (tokens, base, controls, floating, editor, document, writing, settings, dialogs) plus a rewritten src/mobile/mobile.css that only raises geometry tokens and lays out mobile surfaces. legacy.css and the classic palette shim go away.
  2. Color mode and accent come from <html data-theme> like desktop; sheets and dialogs stop carrying their own theme attributes.
  3. Bottom sheets (note, notebook and list actions) are floating surfaces: --bg-elevated, blur, --shadow-pop, 16px top radius, 48px rows. Dialogs stay centered.
  4. Safe areas, the visual-viewport height and keyboard tracking stay as they are; the toolbar, sheets and writing tools keep riding above the keyboard, and the short-landscape rules keep a writing line visible.
  5. Settings: same categories and items as desktop minus desktop-only ones (translucent sidebar, writing width, formatting toolbar, cursor, shortcuts). Search shows labelled results inline.
